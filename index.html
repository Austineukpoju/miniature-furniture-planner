3. Cutting patterns

White-paper blueprint style: black cutting outlines, dimension marks, and labels. Each rectangle is drawn at its real millimetre size. In print preview, choose 100% / Actual size and turn off “Fit to page”. Patterns print one per row so the browser does not shrink them to fit a two-column grid. The SVG includes an 8 mm label margin on each side; very large pieces may exceed the paper and must be printed on larger paper or tiled.

Print-scale check

Measure this line with a ruler after printing at 100%. It should be exactly 100 mm long. If it is not, the print settings or printer scaling are changing the size; do not use the patterns until corrected.

    5. Before you cut your final cardboard

    • Check that your cardboard thickness is reasonably even.
    • Print a paper test at 100% and measure one rectangle with a ruler.
    • Cut slowly with a ruler and a suitable craft knife or scissors; keep fingers clear of the cutting path. Ask an adult for help if needed.
    • Dry-fit the parts before applying glue. If the joints do not sit square, pause and adjust the plan rather than forcing them.
    • Allow glue to dry fully, then check the outside width, height and depth.
